# JLPT N1

N1 is the top JLPT level: logically complex and abstract writing, lectures and
news followed in detail, and comprehension of structure and intent rather than
just content.

## Scope

- **Kanji:** roughly 2,000 characters (broadly the general-use set).
- **Vocabulary:** roughly 10,000 words.
- **Grammar:** literary and archaic-flavoured constructions, dense written
  register, idiom.

## Turning N1 into cards

- Generated decks are not the right tool at N1. A learner here needs real text
  and a record of their own gaps.
- If a deck exists at this band it should be a **mistake log**, not a curriculum.
