# JLPT N3

N3 sits between the basic levels and the advanced ones, and is the level at
which Japanese becomes usable for ordinary life rather than for study. It is the
level Lugha's owner is working toward for a move to Japan.

## Scope

- **Kanji:** roughly 650 characters.
- **Vocabulary:** roughly 3,750 words.
- **Grammar:** keigo in its common forms; passive and causative; a wide range of
  conjunctions and nuance-carrying endings; longer sentences with embedded
  clauses.
- **Listening:** coherent conversation at near-natural speed, following who is
  doing what to whom.

## Turning N3 into cards

- This is where **function beats vocabulary**: cards that teach how to soften a
  refusal, ask for a favour, or check understanding are worth more than another
  noun.
- Keigo deserves its own deck. It is a register, not a tense, and learners
  reliably stall on it.
- Real-talk cards: contracted forms of phrases already known in careful speech.
