# CEFR C1 — fluency without searching

At C1 the language is available without visible effort. The learner follows
implicit meaning, uses the language flexibly for social, academic and
professional purposes, and structures long turns coherently.

## What a C1 learner can do

- Understand long, demanding text and recognise implicit meaning and irony.
- Speak fluently and spontaneously with little obvious searching for words.
- Use the language effectively in professional settings.
- Produce clear, well-structured writing on complex subjects.

## Scope

- **Vocabulary:** 8,000+ words, with a strong sense of which word fits which
  situation.
- **Grammar:** consistently accurate; remaining errors are the kind native
  speakers also make.

## Turning C1 into cards

- Spaced repetition at C1 is for **precision**: near-synonyms, register traps,
  and low-frequency words the learner has decided they need.
- Generated decks at this band are rarely useful. Prefer material the learner
  has met in real text.
