# CEFR C2 — the top band

C2 describes someone who can do essentially anything in the language, summarise
and reconstruct arguments from several sources, and express fine shades of
meaning precisely. It is not "native speaker" — it is a ceiling for the
framework's descriptors, not for a person.

## What a C2 learner can do

- Understand virtually everything heard or read, including rapid speech and
  specialised text.
- Express themselves spontaneously and precisely, differentiating fine shades of
  meaning even in complex situations.

## Turning C2 into cards

- Generated flashcards have little to offer at C2. Real material, real
  conversation and writing do the work.
- If decks are built here at all, they should come from the learner's own
  collected mistakes, not from a level description.
