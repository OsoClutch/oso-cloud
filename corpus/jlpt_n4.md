# JLPT N4

N4 covers basic Japanese. The learner reads everyday topics written in simple
vocabulary and kanji, and follows slow conversation well enough to get the gist.

## Scope

- **Kanji:** roughly 300 characters.
- **Vocabulary:** roughly 1,500 words.
- **Grammar:** the plain (dictionary) form and its uses; te-form and the
  constructions built on it; potential, volitional, and simple conditionals;
  casual speech alongside polite; giving and receiving; transitive and
  intransitive pairs.
- **Listening:** everyday conversation at near-normal speed on familiar topics.

## Turning N4 into cards

- The plain/polite split is the band's real work. Cards should make the learner
  **produce the other register** of a form they already know.
- Transitive and intransitive pairs are the classic confusion: teach them as
  pairs on purpose, not as separate cards weeks apart.
