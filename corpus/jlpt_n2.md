# JLPT N2

N2 is the level most employers in Japan treat as working proficiency. The
learner reads newspapers and general-interest articles and follows news and
conversation at natural speed.

## Scope

- **Kanji:** roughly 1,000 characters.
- **Vocabulary:** roughly 6,000 words.
- **Grammar:** written-register constructions, formal set expressions, and the
  nuance endings that distinguish close meanings.

## Turning N2 into cards

- Decks here are best built from **the learner's own reading**, not from a level
  list. Generated cards tend to be arbitrary at this band.
- Near-synonym discrimination is the highest-value card type.
